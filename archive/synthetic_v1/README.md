# Archived: synthetic-data version (Progress Review II)

These files are kept for transparency. They are **no longer used by the app**.

## What they were

Up to Progress Review II (27.07.2026), the behaviour models were trained on
`data/gaming_dataset_v2.csv`, a **synthetic** dataset of 300 students generated by
`notebooks/generate_enhanced_dataset.py`. Its distributions were parameterised from the
literature (Kappi et al., 2024; Farah et al., 2020; Liu & Zhou, 2024), but no student
answered a questionnaire. The `addiction_level` label was itself a formula of weekly
hours, late-night play, sleep and years of gaming plus random noise.

Results produced from this data therefore describe how well the models recover the
generator's formula, not how well they predict gaming disorder in real students:

| File | Result | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `notebooks/03_model_training_v3.py`, `results/chartA–C` | 14-model comparison, Stacking 83.0% CV accuracy | Synthetic — withdrawn |
| `notebooks/06_ann_cognitive_train.py`, `train_ann_cognitive.py` | ANN attention/memory regressor | Synthetic — withdrawn |
| `notebooks/train_kmeans.py` | 4 K-means gamer archetypes | Synthetic — withdrawn |
| `fusion_evaluation_results.json` | Fusion 76.3% → 79.3% | Synthetic — withdrawn |

The trained model files (`models/*.pkl`, `*.h5`) and the CSV are git-ignored and live in
`models/` and `data/` of this folder on the development machine only.

## What replaced them

- **Behaviour model:** `notebooks/07_train_real_igd_model.py`, trained on real survey data
  from 322 Sri Lankan students with the IGDS9-SF (Manchanayake, 2021, Mendeley Data,
  doi:10.17632/8r2jgm6ygh.1, CC BY 4.0).
- **Cognitive vote:** measured in the app by the research-based test battery, combined as a
  literature prior (Argyriou et al., 2017), because no public dataset pairs these tests with
  gaming-disorder scores.
- **Full-system evaluation:** the Validation Study (`study_mode.py`, `analyze_study.py`).
